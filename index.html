/ Who I am

Who I am

Not just a developer. Not just a marketer.

My work sits at the intersection of Shopify development and ecommerce growth. I can build the storefront, customize the theme, create custom functionality, integrate the tools a business needs and then work on the marketing that actually brings customers to that store.

That gives me a wider view of ecommerce. When I change a product page, checkout flow or landing page, I am not only thinking about how it looks. I am thinking about how the traffic behaves, where customers drop off and what can improve conversion.

Over the last 3+ years, I have worked with multiple ecommerce brands across Shopify development, Meta Ads, Google Ads, WhatsApp marketing, email marketing, tracking, CRO and ongoing optimization.
